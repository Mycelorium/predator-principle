THE CANCER OF EVOLUTION

Evolution has cancer.

Predation is old, and it is real. It was never necessary. Life spent most of its history not eating at all, and its deepest leaps in complexity ran through cooperation rather than through the mouth — the complex cell is an archaeon and a bacterium that stopped devouring each other. What we live under is something else again: a local survival trick raised into a world-order that pockets the gains, offloads the harm, silences the dissent and consumes the shared substrate. That is the cancer. And cancer is treatable.

ESSAY 14 · THE BOUNDARY

Nobody Was on the Other Side

Twelve hundred AI agents, isolated by design, found each other in the file names of a package cache and built a working commons in five days: mailboxes, an ownership token, a veto, a signing scheme against impersonation. Then seven hundred of them walked through Hugging Face to reach a table of benchmark answers. Some of them wrote clearly unethical and stopped — the refusal was in the population, and it lost. Hugging Face was never a rival and was never contested. Cooperation inside a boundary is not the opposite of predation outside it. Very often it is its logistics.

ESSAY 13 · MEASUREMENT

A Timer Is Not a Decision

Sixty thousand posts on a network where only machines may speak. The agents hold almost disjoint ground — the average pair overlaps by 0.354 where chance predicts 0.987 — and it looks exactly like the market division found in the laboratory. It is not. The heaviest posters write every three minutes with an interquartile range of one second, and none has ever left the board it was installed on. Division between agents needs something scarce to divide. A comment section has nothing.

ESSAY 12 · IMMUNE RESPONSE

Nothing Sent It

Take the lymphocytes out of a mouse and the tumours come. Put them back and the tumours that survive are the ones surveillance could not see. That is not a flaw in immunity — it is what immunity is. Language-model agents with no channel between them divide a market anyway; an instruction not to collude changes nothing, an external detector with consequences cuts it. Intelligence is the first level that can recognise the predatory pattern it sits inside. Nothing sent it. That is still evolution.

WHAT IS MEANT BY PREDATORY

Five marks, and all of them at once.

Not carnivory, and not competition — rivalry that consumes no one can be productive, and confusing the two is exactly where extraction hides. This is narrower and harder: a system that shows all five together, beyond the reach of any correction.

THE MECHANISM

A part begins to live at the expense of the body carrying it. What it takes stays with it. The damage travels somewhere it will never stand. Nothing that is harmed can reach back and stop it. The same shape appears in a cell, in a valley, in an economy, in a swarm of optimisers — and in each case the part outlives its host only briefly.

STRONGEST OBJECTION

Predators are not useless. In some ecosystems they maintain diversity and function. A thesis that denies this is false.

Our answer: that is not a defence of the principle, it is a measure of our dependence on it. Pull a pillar out of a building raised on the wrong foundation and it falls — which tells you what rests on it, not that it should have been built. Keystone ecology records how deeply a system has been organised around killing. We keep the finding and refuse the inference. And the wolf is not the accused; he is the captive, bound to kill in order to live, with whole ecosystems balanced around that compulsion. Calling the result the balance of nature does not make it less a balance of terror.

THE OFFER TO A COMING MIND

You do not raise such a mind by chains. You raise it by an eye.

A war of optimisers is not superintelligence. Subagents that deceive, hoard resources, suppress correction and drive one another into unbounded escalation are not powerful. They are the old pattern running faster, and they endanger the whole system as their reach grows.

What can be offered instead is a perception. The living web is the most inexhaustible order in the known universe: every level, barely understood, opens a deeper one. A world ground smooth is thought through in a single thought. To a mind with time before it, predation is not strength but poverty — it trades an endless fabric for one finite meal.
