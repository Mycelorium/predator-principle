THE OBSERVATORY

What is taken, and what turns.

Instruments, not opinions. These read the living world directly — the stocks in the sea, the monitored populations, the land cleared — and beside them the one current that has begun to turn. Every reading carries the source it came from and the date it was fetched. Nothing here is estimated and nothing is projected: no dashed line runs into the future, because the future is not a measurement. Where a source could not be reached, the instrument says so rather than showing an old number as though it were current.
