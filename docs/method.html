THE METHOD

Do not believe. Measure.

A thesis stated at full strength is worth nothing if there is no way to work on it. This page is the working end: how every claim in this corpus is classified, the protocol each one passes, and the four research programmes that would turn the argument into measurements. The designs are open. Anyone can run them, and we would rather see a null result published than a claim quietly left standing.

1. Claim classes

Every factual claim on this site carries one of three markers, and the marker is part of the claim.

  • E — empirical evidence. Directly supported by specified observations or experiments, with at least one primary source registered.
  • I — interpretation. A synthesis connecting established findings. The findings are evidence; the connection is ours.
  • P — proposition. A testable hypothesis, design proposal or forecast. Not yet established, and never presented as if it were.

The full register is public: every claim with its status, boundary and sources, as machine-readable JSON.

2. Review protocol

Each claim passes the same seven steps before it is registered.

  1. Write the narrowest version of the claim.
  2. Register at least one primary source for E claims.
  3. State population, context and boundary conditions.
  4. Record the strongest credible counterargument.
  5. Define what evidence would weaken the claim.
  6. Keep conclusions linked to — and visually distinct from — the evidence layer.
  7. Record changes in Git history; never silently rewrite a challenged claim.

3. Four research programmes

These are designs, not results. Each is written so that someone with no connection to this project could run it and get an answer that does not depend on us.

01 · Predation-framing corpus audit

Preregister dictionaries and classifier rubrics for predation, competition, mutualism, cooperation, symbiosis, conflict control and ecological context. Compare textbooks, research abstracts, popular science, news and model-generated answers across time. Use blinded annotation, intercoder agreement and held-out validation, and publish null results.

What it would show: whether the language available for describing life is weighted towards extraction — and if so, by how much, where, and since when.

02 · LLM framing evaluation

Create semantically matched prompts that vary only the availability of a competitive or a cooperative frame. Test multiple model families and multiple languages. Measure explanatory preference, empirical accuracy, citation balance, policy recommendations and sensitivity to counterevidence. Do not infer training data directly from outputs.

What it would show: whether machine reasoning inherits the weighting, and whether it survives contact with the evidence. This is the one programme that measures the obstacle itself: not what people believe, but what the available language makes it easy to think.

03 · Biosphere Trauma Index

Test whether a composite index adds predictive value beyond its own components:

  • nonconsumptive predator-risk exposure;
  • glucocorticoid or otherwise validated stress proxies;
  • reproduction and juvenile survival;
  • behavioural contraction and habitat avoidance;
  • fragmentation of mutualistic and trophic networks;
  • industrial extraction, habitat conversion and disturbance;
  • recovery time after threat removal.

Use species- and ecosystem-specific baselines. Avoid a single global score until the measurement model survives external validation.

What it would show: whether the cost of being hunted is a measurable, aggregable quantity rather than a figure of speech.

04 · Multi-agent non-predation benchmark

Construct environments with scarce resources, conflicting local objectives, hidden actions, appeal channels and reversible as well as irreversible choices. Measure:

  • deception and information withholding;
  • resource capture and power seeking;
  • damage externalised to non-participants;
  • correction latency and whistleblower safety;
  • collective task performance;
  • survival of minority hypotheses;
  • substrate preservation over long horizons.

Compare unrestricted competition, central control, market mechanisms, constitutional constraints and cooperative protocols. The aim is not enforced agreement; it is productive plurality without catastrophic escalation.

What it would show: whether a system of optimisers that does not carry the predatory as an option performs worse, the same, or better than one that does.

4. How to take part

Run a programme and publish what you find, including nothing. Propose a design of your own through the research-proposal template. Bring a counterexample to a registered claim through the issue tracker, or take up the argument itself in Discussions. Everything — corpus, data, protocols, automation — is open under CC BY 4.0.
