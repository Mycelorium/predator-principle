Earth is not a warehouse

A biosphere is a network of feedback, not an inventory of freely available parts.

Human land use, biomass redistribution, and appropriation of biological production operate at planetary scale. Modern extraction exceeds the reach of a local predator-prey relation.

The Predator Principle therefore asks not only how much is taken. It asks whether those carrying the harm can return effective feedback to the decision.

Evidence boundary · E

Planetary changes have multiple causes and are not explained by predation alone.
