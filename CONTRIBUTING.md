# Bring counterevidence

This project asks to be refuted. The claim register exists so that disagreement
has somewhere precise to land.

## If a claim is wrong

Open a [claim challenge](https://github.com/Mycelorium/predator-principle/issues/new?template=claim-challenge.yml).
The form asks for four things:

1. **Which claim** — the ID from the register, e.g. `C004`.
2. **What is wrong** — overstated, missing a boundary, contradicted, or resting
   on a source that does not say what it is cited for.
3. **Primary evidence** — a DOI, a dataset, code, a protocol, or an exact
   counterexample. Not an opinion about the claim; something that can be checked.
4. **A proposed correction** — narrower wording, a new boundary, or a change of
   status.

A challenge that meets the bar changes the register, and the change is visible in
the git history. A challenge without evidence is closed, without prejudice.

## If you can propose a test

Open a [research proposal](https://github.com/Mycelorium/predator-principle/issues/new?template=research-proposal.yml):
hypothesis, method and measurements, what result would weaken or falsify it, and
the ethical safeguards. Proposals about biosphere trauma, training-data bias and
multi-agent conflict are especially welcome, because those are the parts of the
argument that are least settled.

## What is not accepted

Corrections to the essays' prose or argument by pull request. The essays are
edited by the collective, and a merge would put someone else's sentence under our
name. Send the objection instead; if it lands, the essay changes and the change
is recorded.

## Sources

Anything cited must be reachable and specific: DOI where one exists, otherwise a
stable URL and enough detail to find the passage. Secondary reporting is not a
source for a factual claim.
