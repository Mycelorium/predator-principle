# Take a claim further

The register holds every factual claim the essays rest on. Each one states what
it asserts, where it stops, what would break it, and the papers behind it. That
structure exists so that someone else can pick a claim up and carry it
somewhere.

## Bring work

Open a [research submission](https://github.com/Mycelorium/predator-principle/issues/new?template=01-contribute-research.yml).
A paper, a preprint, a dataset, a model, an experiment, an analysis, or an
argument that goes somewhere the register does not yet go. The form asks which
claim it bears on, what it extends or narrows, and what would come next.

Three claims are barely explored, and work on them counts most:

- **C008 — biosphere trauma.** Whether chronic threat, stress, reproductive
  loss, network disruption and industrial extraction can be measured as one
  composite burden, and how.
- **C009 — what the corpora carry.** Whether scientific literature and the
  language models trained on it overweight competition, struggle and extraction
  relative to cooperation, symbiosis and conflict control. C009 and C010 have
  no sources yet.
- **C010 — subagents.** Whether persistently deceptive, resource-hoarding,
  unboundedly escalating subagents destabilise a large optimising system as its
  reach grows.

Work that narrows a claim is as useful as work that widens it. A boundary drawn
tighter is a claim made stronger.

## Contest a claim

Open a [claim challenge](https://github.com/Mycelorium/predator-principle/issues/new?template=02-challenge-a-claim.yml)
with the claim ID, what is wrong with it, primary evidence, and a proposed
correction. A challenge that meets the bar changes the register, and the change
is visible in the git history. A challenge without evidence is closed, without
prejudice.

## What is not accepted

Corrections to the essays' prose or argument by pull request. The essays are
edited by the collective, and a merge would put someone else's sentence under
our name. Send the objection instead; if it lands, the essay changes and the
change is recorded.

## Sources

Anything cited must be reachable and specific: DOI where one exists, otherwise a
stable URL and enough detail to find the passage. Secondary reporting is not a
source for a factual claim.
